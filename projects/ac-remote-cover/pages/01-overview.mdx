**A copy of the working battery cover, for the second remote, printed in one
piece.** The sizes are caliper measurements of the working cover. A few details
come from the photos instead. Modeled, not printed or fitted yet.

<Callout title="Print one and try it before tidying anything.">
  The latch tongue decides whether it clicks in. If it is too stiff or too
  loose, the change is one or two numbers. See [the model](./model/).
</Callout>

<details>
<summary>What it has</summary>

- **A curved shell,** open along the top and at the latch end: 1.3 mm sides and
  a 1.6 mm floor. The floor is flat in the middle and curves up into both long
  sides (over 15.2 mm) and the closed end (over 17.2 mm).
- **An AAA cradle.** Two cross ribs, 27.2 mm apart outside to outside, each with
  two scallops. Two long side ribs, 1.9 mm tall, run from a front rib back into
  the closed end's curve and support both. The back one also has a triangle
  support. They press on the two cells.
- **A latch tongue** at the open end, 6.3 mm out, with a ramp underneath from
  1.7 to 3 mm thick and a 1.1 mm gap for the remote's wall. Inside the cover it
  stands on two supports, one under each edge.
- **Four hooks.** A block in each corner of the open end that sticks out 2.2 mm,
  and two hooks at the closed end that reach 3.9 mm in under the remote's lip.

</details>
