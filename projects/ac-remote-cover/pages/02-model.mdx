**One printed part: the shell, the cradle ribs, the tongue and the hooks
together.** The tongue is shown in blue and pulls apart on the slider so it is
easy to see. It is printed as one piece with the shell.

## Every size, by code

**Each size the model uses has a code, so a measurement can be read back as
"R7 is 17.0".** Orange sizes are read off a photo or guessed, and are the ones
worth measuring next. Pink ones are worked out from other sizes.

<Drawing name="cover" />

<details>
<summary>How T3, T4, T5 and T11 fit together</summary>

Seen from the side (view 2), the tongue is a flat plate with a ramp hanging
under its outer end.

- **T3, 5.0:** the outside of the floor up to the tongue's top face.
- **T5, 3.0:** the tongue's full thickness at the catch, from its top face down
  to the bottom of the ramp.
- **T4, 1.7:** its thickness at the tip, and everywhere along the plate.
- **T11, 2.0:** the outside of the floor up to the bottom of the catch, T3 minus
  T5. Worked out, not measured.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why R7 is pink</summary>

R7, the closed end to the back rib, is not measured. It follows from the length
(S1), the front rib's position (R1), the rib spacing (R2) and the rib thickness
(R3): 16.6 mm. The curve at the closed end (C2) was measured to end at that rib,
17.2 mm in. The two differ by 0.6 mm, so one of R1 or R2 may be to a different
face of a rib than the model assumes. Measuring R7 directly settles it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Printing it</summary>

- **Open side up,** like the photos. The flat part of the floor sits on the bed.
  The curves underneath need supports: tree supports, touching the build plate
  only.
- **The tongue** bends about 1.3 mm to clear its ramp. On a 14 mm arm that is
  about 2 % strain in the plastic (EST), which PLA takes but not many times over.
  If it cracks, print it in PETG.
- **Masses** in the parts table are for solid plastic. At 100 % infill, which a
  part this thin effectively is, that is the real figure.

</details>
