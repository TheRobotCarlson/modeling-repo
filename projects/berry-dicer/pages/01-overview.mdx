**Pinch the leaves off each berry as you drop it in, then turn a handle: the press
pushes the whole batch through a stainless grid, and a wire cuts it off underneath
every 12 mm.** Everything the fruit touches is
stainless steel and goes in the dishwasher. The printed PLA Pure is frame only.
Modeled, not built.

## Why it works on strawberries

**The awkward shape stops mattering once the leaves are off.** A hulled berry can
land on its side, its tip or its cut face and the grid treats it the same. Pushing
it down 12 mm at a time and cutting underneath after each step gives cubes, not
long sticks. See [how it cuts](./how-it-cuts/).

## How you use it

1. **Pinch the leaves off and drop the berry in the hopper**, about 2 s each (EST).
   Up to 27 berries per batch.
2. **Turn the handle one click, push the wire rod across.** Next click, pull it back.
   A 30 mm berry takes three clicks.
3. **Cubes land in a baking dish underneath.** The last layer stays in the grid until
   the next batch pushes it out.

About 5 minutes for 60 berries a week, washing up included, against about 11 with
a knife (EST). The press wins from about a dozen berries a week. See
[time and force](./physics/).

It is not only for strawberries: anything soft enough to push through by hand
comes out as 12 mm cubes. See [what else it cuts](./how-it-cuts/#what-else-it-cuts).
