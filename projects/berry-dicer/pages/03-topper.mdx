**Lay nine berries on their sides, leafy end against the fence, and swing the arm
down.** The wire goes through every berry and into the slot. Flip the fence flap
forward over the compost to drop the tops, then pick the tray up and tip it into
the press's hopper. The tray is as wide as the hopper.

<details>
<summary>The detail</summary>

- **The arm.** The arm pivots at about half a berry's height. Because of that, the
  wire moves almost straight down through the berries: it drifts about 1.5 mm
  across the cut. It starts above the tallest berry and stops in the slot under the
  bed, where the arms rest on the floor of the base's slots.
- **The fence flap** is the fence and the 5 mm strip in front of the slot, bent from
  one piece of 0.060 in stainless. It hinges at the slot. Shims or a second set of
  hinge holes move it from 3 to 9 mm (not modeled).
- **Stems.** A long stem stub props a berry away from the fence and the cut
  comes out short. Pinch long stems off as you lay them in.
- **The base** is PLA Pure and holds the tray, the pivot posts and the arm stops. It
  never touches fruit. It is hollow underneath to save filament.

</details>
