## Force on the platen

<Chart sim="force" />

**For 27 berries lying in the hopper the platen peaks at about 420 N, if fruit
takes 0.1 N per millimetre of edge to cut (EST).** The force is highest when
the grid is cutting most berries across their widest part. That cutting figure is
the main unknown, which is why the chart shows half and double it. Weighing one berry
pushed through a piece of the grid on a kitchen scale settles it (see
[what to measure](../measure/)).

<Params sim="force" />

<details>
<summary>The model</summary>

- A grid with cells c across, cutting a section of area A, has 2A/c of edge in
  that section. The force is that length times the cutting force per millimetre.
- Each berry lying in the hopper is taken as an ellipsoid, W × W × 1.2 W. The grid's
  top plane cuts each one at the depth it has been pushed in.
- 27 berries, 24 to 36 mm across, random but fixed (seed 4).
- Friction of the cubes in the cells is left out. So is the wire's own cut, which
  runs sideways and is small.
- **At the handle:** the 15.3 mm pinion and 215 mm handle with 80 %
  efficiency give 37 N at the knob.

</details>

## Time each week

<Chart sim="time" />

**At 60 berries a week: about 11 minutes with a knife and 5 with the press,
counting washing up (EST).** The press pays back above about a dozen berries a week.
Most of what is left is pinching the leaves off at 2 s a berry. Every timing here
is a guess to replace with a stopwatch.
