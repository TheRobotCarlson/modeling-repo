**Laser-cut 304 stainless for everything the fruit touches, some stainless wire and
rod, and PLA Pure for the frames. The automated machine adds three steppers, three
servos and a XIAO ESP32S3.** Laser-cut parts are custom, so they have no part
number. Most other rows are still to pick a specific product, so the site marks
them.

<Bom />

The stainless cartridge (hopper, grid, platen and bow) fits both frames, so build it
once.
