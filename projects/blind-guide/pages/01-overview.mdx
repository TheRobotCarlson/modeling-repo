**The bottom bar of each roller shade comes down onto the top of its AXIS Gear
motor and stops there.** The fix is a cap that slides over the top of the motor.
A ramp on its glass side meets the bar first and slides it towards the glass,
where there is a thin gap for it to pass.

<details>
<summary>The detail</summary>

What is known about the installation, from the photos: the motor hangs on the side
of the window recess with its face along the wall, so the glass is beside it. The
shade hangs just beside the motor's glass-side face. Its bottom bar is a little
wider than that gap, so the end of the bar comes down on the edge of the motor's
top, just outside the nearer of the two chain runs.

AXIS's installation guide warns about this case: the shade must never touch the
bracket as it moves past, because contact "impedes shade movement and could lead to
a situation where the shade cannot move past Gear."
