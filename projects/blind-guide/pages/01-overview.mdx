**Two roller shades, each driven by an AXIS Gear motor, catch as they come down.**
The idea is a printed part that slides on top and guides the shade inwards so it
passes cleanly. This page tracks what is known; nothing has been designed or
printed yet.

## What the printer makes possible

The guide can mix materials in one print. The Bambu Lab H3C takes up to nine, and
one of them can be a very soft 75A TPU. That suggests a rigid body for the ramp
with rubber-like TPU where the part has to grip, or where the shade slides over it.

<details>
<summary>The detail</summary>

AXIS's own installation guide warns about exactly this failure: the shade must
never touch the bracket as it moves past, because contact "impedes shade movement
and could lead to a situation where the shade cannot move past Gear." Their test is
to hold a hand where the bracket will go and run the shade past it. A guide is the
fix for an installation where that clearance was not available.

</details>
