**The AXIS Gear is a 46 × 38 × 165 mm box that hangs on a wall bracket and pulls
the shade's bead chain around a cogwheel.** Use the slider to take it apart: the
chain comes in through the top, wraps under the cogwheel behind the back cover, and
the bracket's two lips sit in grooves low on the back.

## Where the numbers come from

| Dimension | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Body width × depth × height | 46 × 38 × 165 mm | Manufacturer's dimension drawing |
| "Gear dimensions" on the box | 43 × 47 × 169 mm | Box label |
| Depth including bracket | 47 mm | Inferred: the box's 47 minus the drawing's 38 leaves 9 mm for the bracket |
| Weight | 314 g | Box label |
| Solar bar | 406 × 36 × 23 mm, 490 g | Box label (drawing says 408 mm) |
| Space needed below the bracket | 3 in (76 mm) | Installation guide, for the power plug |
| Everything else | — | Scaled from drawings and photos |

**The box and the drawing disagree, by 3 mm in width and 4 mm in height.** The model
uses the drawing. A caliper reading of the real unit settles it.

<summary>The detail</summary>

How it mounts, from the installation guide:

- The bracket fixes to the frame with two screws or double-sided tape, either on
  its back (inside mount) or on its side (outside and mullion mounts).
- The back cover comes off, the chain goes over the cogwheel, and the cover snaps
  back. The motor will not power on with the cover off.
- The motor goes onto the bracket at an angle so the lips enter the grooves, then
  slides **down** until the chain is taut. A ratchet holds it there.
- To remove it, pull the bottom about a centimetre away from the bracket and lift.
- Power (solar bar or adapter) plugs into the bottom.

What that means for a guide: it cannot block the chain where it enters the top,
it should not cover the three buttons on the top face unless they stay reachable,
and it must still let the motor lift off its bracket.

Sources: AXIS Gear Partner Course Documentation v1.4 (September 2019), the
[installation](https://support.helloryse.com/en/articles/5193936-how-to-install-axis-gear)
and [mounting](https://support.helloryse.com/en/articles/5193923-mounting-dismounting-axis-gear-from-its-bracket)
articles on the Ryse support site, and the product box.
