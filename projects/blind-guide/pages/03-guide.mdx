**The guide is a 13 g cap that drops over the top of the motor, with a 60° ramp
along its glass-side edge.** The bar meets the ramp above the motor instead of the
motor's flat top, slides down it towards the glass, and carries on down a 1.2 mm
skin on the motor's side.

<ModelViewer assembly="guide" height={560} />

## How it works

1. **Ramp.** It starts 4 mm below the motor's top on the glass side and rises
   inwards to a crest 6.7 mm above the top. Anything landing within 4.7 mm of the
   motor's edge lands on the slope.
2. **Skin.** The glass-side wall is 1.2 mm thick, to fit the thin gap beside the
   motor. Its lower edge is feathered so the bar does not hook it on the way back up.
3. **Chain slots.** Two slots, open to the back, let the cap go on around the chain
   without unthreading it. The ramp stops just short of the nearer chain run.
4. **Grip pads.** Two soft TPU pads take up the clearance so the cap stays put.

## Two materials, for opposite reasons

**The ramp must be slippery and the grip must not be.** The body, ramp included, is
rigid PETG. The 75A TPU goes only on the two pads that press against the motor. A
TPU ramp would be the wrong way round: see [will it slide](./physics/).

## Printing

Print it front face down. The part is one outline pulled straight along the motor's
depth, so in that orientation it needs no support, and the layer lines run along
the direction the bar slides rather than across it.

<details>
<summary>The detail</summary>

- Clearance to the motor is 0.3 mm a side. The pads sit 0.5 mm into the wall and
  stand 0.3 mm proud of it, so they just touch the motor.
- The front lip is 14 mm tall and stops above the power button.
- The cap covers the Set, Group and Pair buttons on the motor's top. Lift it off to
  reach them.
- The top plate and ramp carry on 8.5 mm behind the motor, over the wall mount.
  That assumes nothing behind the motor stands higher than its top.
- The motor still lifts off its bracket with the cap on, since the back is open.

What would change the design: a bar that overhangs the motor by more than 4.7 mm
runs into the chain, and the ramp cannot follow it there. A gap beside the motor
narrower than about 1.5 mm will not take the skin.
