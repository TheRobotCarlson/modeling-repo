title: What to measure
summary: The guide cannot be designed until these are known.
---

**The model so far is the motor alone. The guide depends on the shade, and none of
that is measured yet.**

## About the catch

- What catches: the bottom bar of the shade, the fabric edge, or the chain?
- What it catches on: the top of the motor, its side, the bracket, or something
  else on the frame?
- Which way is "inwards": towards the glass, or towards the middle of the window?
- A photo of each shade stopped at the point where it catches would answer most of this.

## About the shade

| Measurement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Roller tube diameter, with the fabric fully rolled and fully unrolled | Sets where the fabric hangs relative to the motor |
| Bottom bar: width, depth, and how far its end is from the motor | The bar is the usual thing that snags |
| Gap between the fabric edge and the motor, at the height of the motor's top | How much the guide has to move the shade |
| Gap between the fabric and the wall or frame behind it | Whether there is room to push it "inwards" |
| Bead chain: bead diameter and spacing | The guide must not pinch the chain |

## About the motor

| Measurement | Model assumes |
|---|---|
| Body width, depth, height | 46 × 38 × 165 mm |
| Front corner radius | 7 mm |
| Where the chain leaves the top: distance between the two runs, and from the back | 31 mm apart, 9 mm from the back |
| Bracket: width, height, depth | 46 × 62 × 9 mm |
| How it is mounted: inside, outside or mullion | Unknown |
