title: What to measure
summary: Three numbers decide whether the first print fits.
---

**The design rests on three guesses about the window, and several about the motor.**
A ruler and a set of calipers settle all of them.

## At the window

| Measurement | Design assumes | If it is different |
|---|---|---|
| How far the bottom bar overhangs the motor's top, from the glass-side edge | Up to 4.5 mm | Past 4.7 mm the bar is over the chain; the ramp needs rethinking |
| Gap between the motor's glass-side face and whatever is beside it | At least 1.5 mm | The skin is 1.2 mm; a tighter gap needs a thinner one |
| Does anything behind the motor stand higher than its top? | No | The cap's back extension would have to be cut short |
| Is the second motor the same way round, or mirrored? | Unknown | Both versions are on [the guide](../guide/) page |
| Distance from the roller down to the motor's top | 150 mm or more | Only matters for the friction check, and barely |

## On the motor

| Measurement | Model assumes |
|---|---|
| Body width, depth, height | 46 × 38 × 165 mm |
| Radius of the top corners, seen from the front | 7 mm |
| Chain: distance between the two runs, and from the motor's back | 31 mm apart, 9 mm from the back |
| Chain bead diameter | 4.5 mm |
| Distance from the top down to the power button | 16 mm |

The fastest check of all of these at once is to print the cap and try it.
