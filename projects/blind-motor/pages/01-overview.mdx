**A printed box on the wall pulls each shade's bead chain with a worm-gear motor, and always knows where the shade is.**
A worm gear can't be turned backwards, so the chain can't pull the motor round: the shade
holds still with the power off, and nobody can pull it out of step. An encoder on the motor
counts every turn it makes. Two 18650 cells keep the count running through power cuts, and
USB-C keeps them charged. Home Assistant sees each shade as a cover with a position slider.

## Why a worm gear, and why it knows where it is

**Three things that go wrong with a stepper all go away.**

1. **Hand pulls:** a stepper can be pulled round from the chain, and the count is wrong.
   A worm gear can't be turned from its output, so the chain is locked unless the motor runs.
2. **Skipped steps:** a stepper that slips under load doesn't know. A DC motor with an encoder
   counts what it actually did.
3. **Power cuts:** the cells keep the ESP32 counting, and the count is saved to flash within
   10 s of every change. A flat battery and a power cut together lose at most a move in progress.

The trade-off: with the chain locked, the shade only moves by motor. The box has its own up and
down buttons for that, and lifting the chain off the sprocket frees it.

<details>
<summary>What it borrows from the existing designs</summary>

- **The sprocket idea and size** from [bead-chain-blind-motor](https://github.com/jamesrklau/bead-chain-blind-motor):
  12 pockets on a 6 mm-pitch chain, 72 mm a turn, a D bore and a set screw.
- **The motor type** from the MakerWorld [Smart Roller Blind Motor](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3292116),
  which uses a 12 V worm-gear motor. It only times the motor, so it has no idea where the shade is;
  this one counts.
- **The overall size** from the AXIS Gear it replaces: 165 × 38 × 46 mm by its listing. This box is
  taller and wider but shallower, because of the cells.

Those designs, and the others considered, are on [the build](./build/) page.

</details>

<details open>
<summary>What to measure first</summary>

- **Where the chain hangs:** the distance from the side wall the box goes on to the chain's line.
  The box puts it **B4 = 38.2 mm** out. More can be made up with a spacer behind the box; less
  needs a thinner box.
- **The bead chain:** bead diameter (C1) and bead pitch (C2), with calipers over 10 beads.
- **The pull:** a luggage scale on the chain, lifting the shade from fully down. The motor's
  listing gives 5.6 kg·cm at 12 V, about 4.8 kg of pull at the sprocket. On the cells it runs at about
  8 V, so roughly two-thirds of that.

</details>
