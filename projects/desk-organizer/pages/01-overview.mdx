**The recommended layout is three printed modules on a 4 × 3 grid, 6.6 × 5.0 in.
That is about the size of the container you use now, with a pocket for every
thing.** Each thing stands at least half out of its pocket so you can grab it. The
USB stick and YubiKey drop into rounded basins you can sweep them out of. Modeled,
not printed.

## Three ways to group the same pockets

**Fewer, bigger prints mean fewer walls and a smaller footprint, at the cost of
swapping things around later.** All three fit the 9 × 7 in space. See
[variants](./variants/) to turn each one in 3D.

| | Modular | Combined (recommended) | One block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Footprint | 6.6 × 6.6 in (168 mm) | 6.6 × 5.0 in (168 × 126 mm) | 6.4 × 3.9 in (163 × 100 mm) |
| Prints | Plate and 4 modules | Plate and 3 modules | 1 |
| Free for later | 3 cells | 1 cell, and room for a 5th column | None |
| Gridfinity | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change one thing | Reprint one small module | Reprint one module | Reprint everything |

<details>
<summary>What was combined</summary>

- **The pen, brush and metal stick go round the glasses case.** The triangle leaves
  three empty corners in its square bin, and each takes one of them. This is the
  same in every variant.
- **The anything bowl is gone.** The two basins take the loose things.
- **Combined:** the AirPods and both basins share one 2 × 2 hub. That saves a row
  over the modular layout.
- **One block:** everything in one 35 mm tall print, with 2.4 mm between pockets
  and no grid. It is the smallest, but nothing can be added or moved.

</details>
