## Combined, 4 × 3 (recommended)

**Three modules on a 168 × 126 mm plate: the grip stand across the back, the
glasses stand and the AirPods hub in front.** One cell at the back right is free.
A fifth column (210 mm, 8.3 in) still fits the desk space if more turns up.

## One block

**Every pocket in one 163 × 100 × 35 mm print, no grid.** The back band has the grip
trainer and the AirPods. The front band has the glasses case (with the pen,
brush and stick round it) and the two basins. It is the most compact, but it is
not modular: a new thing means a new block.
