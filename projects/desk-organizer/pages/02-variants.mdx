## One block

**Every pocket in one 186 × 100 × 35 mm print, no grid.** The back band has the grip
trainer and the AirPods. The front band has the glasses case (with the pen,
brush and stick round it), the two basins, and the two tools one behind the
other at the right-hand end. It is the most compact, but it is
not modular: a new thing means a new block.
