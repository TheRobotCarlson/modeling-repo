---
title: Pockets
summary: What each pocket holds, how deep it is, and how much of the item stands proud.
---

**Every pocket is the item's size plus 0.6 mm a side, and the same in all three
variants.** Most are shallow enough that the item stands well above them. The
small loose things drop into rounded basins instead.

| Pocket | Holds | Size and depth | Stands proud |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glasses case | Triangular case on its end, flat side to the front | 73 mm triangle, 18 mm deep, corner reliefs | 137 of 155 mm (EST) |
| Pen | One small pen, upright | 11.2 mm round, 30 mm deep | 80 of 110 mm (EST) |
| Brush | Keyboard brush on its 0.8 × 0.3 in base | 21.5 × 8.8 mm slot, 30 mm deep | 120 of 150 mm (EST) |
| Metal stick | Upright | 6.3 mm round, 30 mm deep | 46 of 76 mm |
| Grip trainer | Standing on its 4 × 1 in base | 103 × 27 mm slot, 30 mm deep | 80 of 110 mm (EST) |
| AirPods | AirPods 4 case, upright, lid up | 51 × 22 mm rounded, 20 mm deep | 26 of 46 mm |
| Swiss army knife | On its end, 20 × 23 mm | 21.2 × 24.2 mm rounded, 30 mm deep | 60 of 90.5 mm |
| Leatherman | On its end, 13.6 × 27 mm | 14.8 × 28.2 mm rounded, 30 mm deep | 72 of 101.8 mm |
| Basins (two) | USB stick, YubiKey, anything small | 38 mm wide, 13 to 15 mm deep, rounded floor | — |

<details>
<summary>The detail</summary>

- **Round the glasses case.** The triangle sits in a square bin, which leaves
  three corners empty. The pen and the brush go in the two back corners, either
  side of the case's point. The stick goes in the gap beside its right-hand face.
  All three go 30 mm deep, deeper than the case's 18 mm, so they do not wobble.
- **Basins.** Rounded floors (7 to 9 mm radius) and rounded corners: a key slides
  to the middle, and a fingertip pushes it up the curve and out.
- **The two tools** stand side by side in a 1 × 1 bin with 2.4 mm between them.
  Together they take 38.4 of the 39.1 mm inside, so the outer walls are 1.5 mm.
  The pocket corners are rounded (EST radius); a tool with square corners still
  drops in, but a wider one does not.
- **Corner reliefs.** A printed inside corner is never as sharp as the glasses
  case's edge, so each corner of the triangle has a small round relief.
- **The AirPods case** stands with the lid up, so it can be opened in place. Its
  plan-view corner radius (9.5 mm) is estimated.
- **Masses** in the parts tables are for solid plastic. Printed at 15 % infill
  they are about a third of that (EST).

</details>
