**The combined layout is now full, but a fifth column still fits the desk
space.** Growing the plate from 4 × 3 to 5 × 3 makes it 210 mm (8.3 in) wide and
adds three cells. The modular layout has two free cells. The
one block has none.

<details>
<summary>Three ways to fill a cell</summary>

1. **A ready-made Gridfinity bin.** Search Printables or MakerWorld for
   "Gridfinity" and the thing (cable, SD card, earbuds, phone stand). Bins with or
   without a stacking lip both fit.
2. **A new module here.** In `cad/organizer.py`, add the item's size, a builder
   that cuts its pocket from `bin_block(nx, ny, units)`, and an entry in `MODULES`
   of the variant, with the free cell it takes. The tests check that it does not
   overlap another module, that the item clears its pocket, and that everything
   fits the desk space and the bed.
3. **Rearrange.** A module's cell is one line in `MODULES`. Move a module into a
   free cell, or mirror the layout if you are left-handed.

</details>

## Growing the plate

**A second baseplate of any size sits beside the first.** Bins that straddle the
seam hold the two together, as on any Gridfinity desk. The H3C prints up to 7 × 7
cells (294 mm) in one plate. The desk space allows up to 5 × 4
(210 × 168 mm). The plate size is the `grid` of the variant in `cad/organizer.py`.
