---
title: What to measure
summary: A few sizes are guesses; one small test print checks the fit on your printer.
---

**Check these with a ruler, and print one small test, before printing the
set.** Each change is one number at the top of `cad/organizer.py`.

| What | Model uses | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Grip trainer: height, standing | 110 mm (EST); base 4 × 1 in | Only how much stands proud; the slot is 30 mm deep |
| Glasses case length | 155 mm (EST) | Only how much stands proud; the pocket stays 18 mm deep |
| AirPods model | AirPods 4, 50.1 × 21.2 × 46.2 mm (Apple) | AirPods 2nd gen is 44.3 × 21.3 × 53.5 mm, taller and narrower: a different pocket |
| Pen | 10 mm round, 110 mm long (EST) | The hole is 11.2 mm; a thicker pen needs a bigger one |
| Brush length | 150 mm (EST); base 0.8 × 0.3 in | Only how much stands proud |
| Metal stick | 0.2 in round, 3 in long | If it is flat, the hole becomes a slot |
| USB stick and YubiKey thickness | 8 mm and 3.3 mm (EST) | Only for the picture: the basins are 13 to 15 mm deep |

## Test print first

1. **The glasses stand** (2 × 2). Four fits in one: the case, the pen, the brush
   and the stick. Print a 2 × 2 corner of the baseplate with it to check that it
   drops in and lifts out cleanly. That checks the Gridfinity foot and the 0.6 mm
   pocket clearance together.
2. **The rest**, once those fit.
