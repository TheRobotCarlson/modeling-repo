---
title: What to measure
summary: The guesses in the model, and the first prints and tests that settle them.
---

**The kit's outline is published; almost everything else inside is a stand-in.**
Calipers on the real parts, a test print of the keypad, and a power meter settle
the rest.

## Before printing

| Part | Model assumes | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Kit stack height, XIAO on Wio board | 8.4 mm including USB-C | Sets the floor-to-top clearance |
| USB-C port: height above the bottom of the stack, overhang past the board | 7.2 mm, 1.0 mm | Lines up with the hole in the wall |
| XIAO charge LED position | Near the USB end | The light window above it |
| Battery cell | 33 × 20 × 5 mm with its protection board | Pocket and stops on the base |
| 12 mm switch height (puck) | 7.3 mm | Stem gap, 0.2 mm |
| 6 mm switch height (bar) | 5.0 mm | Stem gap, 0.2 mm |
| FPC antennas | 35 × 9 and 45 × 10 mm | Fit on the wall |
| Where the battery pads are with the Wio board fitted | Reachable | Wiring |

The stem gaps are the numbers to get right. Too large, and the button rolls its
channel without clicking. Too small, and the switch is pressed all the time and
the remote never sleeps. Both show up on the first try.

## First prints

1. **A keypad coupon.** One cap in a 40 mm square of shell with its TPU channel.
   Prints in minutes. Checks that the TPU bonds to PLA, that the channel bridges,
   and how the press feels.
2. **The base alone.** Drop the kit, battery and switch in. Checks the cradles and
   the switch height.
3. **The full puck.**

## After building

| Test | How | Model says |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep current | µA meter in the battery lead | ~20 µA, not counting self-discharge |
| Press, Wi-Fi | USB power meter, or a scope on a shunt | ~3 s awake, ~0.08 mAh |
| Press to event in Home Assistant | Stopwatch, or the logbook | ~2.5 s |
| Radio asleep | Sleep current with and without the cold-sleep line | ~0.6 mA difference |
| Wake on every button | Press each one, watch the event's `button` | 1 to 5 |
| Wi-Fi and LoRa range with the antennas inside | Walk around with it | Unknown |
