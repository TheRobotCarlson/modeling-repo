**Besides the kit: a cell, switches, a vibration motor with its transistor and diode,
five resistors and a capacitor per remote, an accelerometer for the puck, four
screws, and some wire.** Each row links to where to buy it. Quantities are for one
puck, one bar and the gateway. Prices are filled in only where the page showed one; DigiKey and
McMaster prices depend on quantity.

The CAD uses the sizes of these exact parts: the cell's listed dimensions, and the
switch heights from Omron's datasheet. A different cell or switch means changing
`cad/_parts.py`.

<Bom />

Printing takes four materials and up to seven colours: PLA for the shell, base and
caps, clear PLA for the light pipe, 75A TPU for the keypad and feet, and a
dissolvable support. A little heat-shrink sleeves the motor driver.

The [gateway](../gateway/) is a third kit with an optional SHT40, and prints in the same
materials. The optional [sensor module](../sensor-module/) has its own parts list.
