**Besides the kit: a cell, switches, two resistors and a capacitor per remote, four
screws, and some wire.** Each row links to where to buy it. Quantities are for one
puck and one bar. Prices are filled in only where the page showed one; DigiKey and
McMaster prices depend on quantity.

The CAD uses the sizes of these exact parts: the cell's listed dimensions, and the
switch heights from Omron's datasheet. A different cell or switch means changing
`cad/_parts.py`.

<Bom />

Printing takes three materials and up to six colours: PLA for the shell, base and
caps, 75A TPU for the keypad and feet, and a dissolvable support.
