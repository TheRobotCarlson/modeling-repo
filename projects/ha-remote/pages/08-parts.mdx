**Besides the kit, a cell, some switches and two resistors per remote.** None of the
generic parts has been chosen yet, so their rows are open. Quantities are for one
puck and one bar.

Printing takes three materials and up to six colours: PLA for the shell, base and
caps, 75A TPU for the keypad and feet, and a dissolvable support.
